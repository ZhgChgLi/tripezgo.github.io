Why it exists

The hard part isn’t choosing where to go. It’s the order.

Build it in a spreadsheet and you do the arithmetic yourself, look up every connection yourself, paste in every ticket yourself. Then three things happen.

01

It scatters

The plan in a notes app, the flight in email, the hotel confirmation in a chat thread, the restaurant in a screenshot. On the ground, finding the ticket takes longer than getting to the place.

02

The timing is guesswork

How long that walk takes, when the next train leaves — you look each one up by hand. Miss a single leg and the rest of the day goes with it.

03

Plans always change

A closure, a stop that ran long, traffic. Changing it is such a chore that most people don’t — and from day two the plan in your hand isn’t true any more.

Features

Everything a trip needs, in one app

No switching between a calendar, a map, a notes app and your camera roll.

Events on a calendar, travel on a timeline

Every day is a timeline: long-press and drag to move an event or change its times. The gap between two events is a leg — walking, driving and cycling times are estimated from the map, and a transit leg can hold several departures, so you can switch if you miss one. If a leg takes longer than the gap you left, you're warned in advance. All-day things like a rental car get their own header, cancelled events stay in place, struck through, and you can shift the rest of the day earlier or later in one go. There are 56 built-in event types, and you can add your own.

Overview: itinerary, files and to-dos on one page

Each trip splits into Itinerary, Files and To-Dos, so you can check everything is ready before you leave. Tickets, bookings, PDFs, photos and links attach to their event and open with one tap; documents for the whole trip sit at the top. To-dos can belong to the whole trip or to a single event.

Daily routes on the map, shops and walking directions

Every event with a place is on the map. View one day or the whole trip, with each day's events joined in order, and walking, driving and cycling legs drawn along the actual route. Shops in Japan are on the map too — 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson, Don Quijote, Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Daikoku Drug, Bic Camera and Yodobashi, with opening status — with turn-by-turn walking navigation in the app, or a hand-off to Apple Maps or Google Maps. Long-press the map to drop your own pin, or share a place in from another app.

Notes and shopping list, for life on the road

Snap a photo to add a shopping item, record each shop's price under it, and see at a glance which is cheapest. Currency conversion works offline with the last rates it fetched. Notes belong to a trip and can be kept private from your companions, and built-in Japanese travel phrases read themselves aloud with one tap. Share links, photos and files from other apps straight into a note, the shopping list, the trip's files or a map pin.

Plan together, see each other on the map

Send one invite link and your companions join the trip; everyone edits the same itinerary, notes and shopping list. During the trip, turn on Share My Location and your companions appear on the map, with how long ago each was updated. It's shared only with people on this trip, only while it's under way, and you can turn it off any time.

Live Activities, widgets and calendar integration Premium

While the trip is under way, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the current event, the time left, the next stop and how you're getting there. Widgets cover today's events, files, to-dos, shopping list, currency, notes and more, plus Lock Screen widgets; tick off to-dos and shopping items right on the widget (iOS 17 or later). Write the trip into Apple Calendar or Google Calendar and it stays up to date as you edit, and Siri Shortcuts can tell you your next event or today's plan.

Live Activities and calendar integration are part of Premium.

Works offline, private by default

No account, no server of ours

View and edit your trips with no connection. Your data is stored on this device and synced between your own devices through your own iCloud account — never kept on a third-party server.

  • No sign-up. Install it and plan day one — no account, no password.
  • Your trip content stays yours. The app uses Firebase only for crash reports and basic usage statistics, which don't include your trips. Ads in the free version come from Google AdMob, and whether they may track you is your choice in the iOS prompt. Premium has no ads.
  • Sync runs on your iCloud. Data goes into your Apple ID’s private database; only your devices can open it.
  • Sharing runs on iCloud too. Sharing a trip uses Apple’s own iCloud sharing; the invite link is simply the key, and it never passes through a server of ours.
  • Backup and export are free. Back up a whole library to one file, or export a trip as .ics.
