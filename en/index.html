Features

Everything a trip needs, in one app

No more switching between a calendar, a maps app, a notepad and a photo album.

Calendar, map and overview — a whole day that fits

Arrange each day and the route between stops however you like. The gap between two stops? It works that one out for you. Drag to reorder, and a day carries its events with it.

See what’s nearby, add it to today

How far, how long on foot — then drop it straight into the day you’re already planning. Not sure which day yet? Save it as a marker and it stays on the map until you decide.

Tickets, to-dos, notes — all on that one stop

No hunting for the PDF: it lives under the event, ready the moment you arrive. Documents that don’t belong to any single day — passports, insurance — go in Important Files.

Can’t say it out loud? Tap and it speaks

Common travel phrases built in, with romanization and translation. Just play it for them. Speech is synthesised on the device, so it works with no connection.

Your next move, on the Lock Screen

Widgets, Live Activities, Apple Calendar and Siri Shortcuts — fewer taps while you travel. The event you’re in shows up on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you know what’s next without opening the app.

Offline-first, privacy-first

No account. No server of ours.

View and edit trips with no connection. Your travel data lives on this device and syncs, encrypted, through your own iCloud account — never through a third-party server.

  • No sign-up. Install it and plan day one. No account, no password.
  • No tracking, no analytics SDK. There is no third-party analytics or advertising code in the app.
  • Sync runs on your iCloud. Data is stored in your Apple ID’s private database — only your devices can open it.
  • Backup and export are free. Archive a whole vault into one file, and export any trip as an .ics calendar.
