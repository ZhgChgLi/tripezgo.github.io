1. Scope

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how the TripEZGo mobile application (the “App”) and the official website tripezgo.com (the “Site”) handle information. The App is developed and published by ZHONG CHENG LI (“we”, “us”).

By downloading, installing or using the App or the Site, you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

2. Information we handle

The App is designed to operate on your device.

2.1 Content you provide

Content you create, enter or import in the App (such as plans, text entries, checklist items, attached files and images) is stored on your device and may, depending on your settings, be synced and backed up through your own cloud account. That content belongs to you and we claim no ownership of it.

2.2 Information we do not collect

  • The App has no account registration or sign-in. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information.
  • We do not collect, use or share your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not collect your content for analytics, statistics or any commercial purpose.

2.3 Information you send us directly

If you contact us by email, we receive whatever you include in that message (such as your address and a description of your issue). We use it only to respond to and handle your enquiry.

3. Device permissions

Depending on its features, the App may ask for permission to access certain device capabilities or data (for example location, calendar, notifications, photos or files). The specific permissions requested may change between versions as features change.

The following principles apply to all permissions:

  • Every permission is optional. You may decline any of them; the rest of the App continues to work and only the corresponding feature is unavailable.
  • Permissions are used solely to provide the corresponding feature you are using, and for no other purpose.
  • Data obtained through a permission is, as a rule, processed only on your device. It is not transmitted to us and is not provided to third parties for marketing or analytics.
  • You can review and change granted permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. How information is used

Information handled by the App is used only to provide, maintain and improve the App’s functionality; to sync and back up your content according to your settings; to process your purchases and entitlement status; and to respond to your enquiries and provide necessary technical support.

5. Where your data is stored

Your content is stored on your device, and — if you enable sync or backup — also in your own cloud account (for example the iCloud storage associated with your Apple ID).

We do not operate servers that receive or hold your content, and we cannot access, read or delete data stored in your cloud account. That data is held by your cloud provider under its own terms and privacy policy, and remains under your control.

6. Third-party services

The App relies on system services provided by the operating system and platform vendor (Apple) to deliver its features, which may include cloud sync and backup, maps and place information, calendar, speech, widgets and in-app purchases. Your interactions with those services are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The App does not include third-party software development kits (SDKs) whose purpose is behavioural tracking, advertising or user analytics. The third-party components used may change between versions, but this principle does not.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

We do not provide your content to third parties, except: (a) with your consent or at your direction (for example when you use a share or export feature yourself); (b) where required by law, court order or a competent authority; or (c) where necessary to protect the legitimate rights, property or personal safety of us or others.

8. Network connections

The App’s core features are designed to work offline. The App may connect to the network in order to sync or back up to your cloud account, to provide map or location-related features, to process purchases and restores, and — when you paste or share an external link yourself — to retrieve preview information from the site that link belongs to.

These requests are made directly by your device and do not pass through any server of ours. Third-party sites or services that receive such requests handle that information under their own privacy policies.

9. Purchases and payment

All in-app purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information (such as card numbers, billing addresses or Apple ID credentials); that information is handled entirely by Apple.

The App receives from the platform only the status information necessary to determine whether you hold the relevant entitlement, and keeps it on your device. It is never sent to us.

10. Retention and deletion

Because we do not hold your content, retention and deletion are entirely under your control:

  • You can delete individual content in the App at any time. Some items may first move to a trash or holding area and be permanently removed after a period; you may also delete them permanently yourself at any time.
  • Deleting the App removes the data stored on that device.
  • Data held in your cloud account can be deleted by you in that cloud service’s settings, or by turning sync off in the App. Only you can perform this.

11. Security

Your data is protected by the operating system’s sandboxing and file protection mechanisms. Data synced to the cloud is encrypted by that cloud service and held in your private storage.

Please note that no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure. If you export or share a backup file to another service, that copy leaves the protections described above and its security is your responsibility and that of the service you chose.

12. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the App collects no personal data, we hold none belonging to users of any age. If you believe otherwise, please contact us.

13. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable laws, you have rights to access, obtain a copy of, correct, supplement, restrict the processing of, and delete personal data.

For this App, you exercise those rights directly: your content exists only on your device and in your own cloud account, so you can view, edit, export or delete it at any time without submitting a request to us. We neither sell nor “share” personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

If you have questions or requests regarding this Policy, you may still contact us under section 17.

14. This website

This website is a static site. It does not set cookies of its own, embed analytics, or use tracking pixels. As with any website, the hosting provider may record standard server access logs for operational and security purposes; that is governed by the provider’s own privacy policy, and we do not access or analyse those logs.

15. Changes to the app

The App’s features, interface and the system services it uses may be added to, modified or removed in future versions. The handling principles described in this Policy apply across versions; any feature or service mentioned here is illustrative only and does not constitute a commitment to provide, maintain or continue any particular feature.

16. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time. Revisions are reflected in the “Last updated” date on this page; where changes are material, we will indicate this in the App or on this website by appropriate means. Continuing to use the App after a change is published constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
