1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the TripEZGo iOS application (the “App”) and the official website tripezgo.com (the “Site”) handle information. The App is developed and published by ZhgChgLi, an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

By using the App or the Site, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

2. What we do not collect

To avoid any ambiguity, here is what we do not do:

  • The App has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials.
  • We operate no servers that receive, store, or process your travel data.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs — no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no AppsFlyer, none of it.
  • We do not track you for advertising and perform no “tracking” as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. The App never asks for tracking permission, because it does not track.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone — we have none of your data to sell.
  • The App does not collect your location in the background and never records a location history.

3. Where your data lives

Content you create in the App exists in exactly two places:

  1. Locally on your device. Data is stored in the App’s sandbox and App Group container, protected by iOS Data Protection and your device passcode / Face ID / Touch ID.
  2. Your own private iCloud database. If you are signed in to an Apple ID with iCloud enabled, the App uses Apple’s CloudKit to store backup snapshots in the private database of your own Apple ID, for cross-device sync and backup. That data sits in your own iCloud storage, counts against your own iCloud quota, and the developer cannot access, read, or delete it.

The App handles the following kinds of content, all of which you enter or import yourself:

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in the App’s settings. With sync off, your data stays only on that device.

5. When the app uses the network

The App’s core features work fully offline: viewing, adding and editing trips needs no connection. These are the only four situations in which the App makes a network request:

SituationConnects toWhat is sent
iCloud sync and backup Apple iCloud / CloudKit Your trip backup snapshot, transmitted encrypted to the private database of your own Apple ID
Searching for places, resolving addresses, loading map tiles Apple Maps (MapKit) The search terms you type and related coordinates. These requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the App keeps no search history and we never see these requests
Adding a web or map link through the Share sheet The website that the shared link belongs to The App requests that URL in order to expand short links and read the page title and preview image (Open Graph data). This happens only when you share something yourself, as a one-off request; the result comes straight back to your device and never passes through us
Purchases, restoring purchases, fetching prices Apple App Store (StoreKit) Handled by Apple — see section 7

The App’s text-to-speech feature uses the speech synthesiser built into iOS. It runs on your device, needs no connection, and sends no text to any server.

6. Third-party services and SDKs

The only third-party library bundled in the App is SnapKit, an open-source layout library. It makes no network requests, collects no data, and contains no tracking code.

Beyond that, the App uses only Apple’s own system frameworks and services: iCloud/CloudKit, MapKit, EventKit (Calendar), StoreKit (purchases), ActivityKit (Live Activities), WidgetKit (widgets) and AVFoundation (speech). Your interactions with those Apple services are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Purchases and payment

All purchases are made through the Apple App Store. We never see your payment information — card numbers, billing addresses and Apple ID details are handled entirely by Apple and are not available to us.

What the App receives from Apple StoreKit is limited to whether this Apple ID currently holds a Premium entitlement, along with renewal or trial dates, which is used to decide whether to unlock paid features. That information is cached on your device and is never sent to us.

Subscription management, payment method changes and refund requests are all handled in your App Store account.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold none of your data, deletion is entirely in your hands:

  • Delete individual content: delete it in the App. Deleted trips go to the trash and are kept for 30 days before being permanently removed; you can also delete them permanently at any time.
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the App and its local data goes with it.
  • Delete data in iCloud: go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, find TripEZGo and delete its data; or simply turn iCloud sync off in the App. Only you can perform this.

9. Export and portability

The App offers two free export paths so your data can always leave with you:

  • Full backup (.tezbak): packages an entire vault — data, preferences and files — into a single file you can save to the Files app or anywhere you choose.
  • Calendar export (.ics): exports a trip’s events in the standard calendar format for import into other calendar software.

10. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS Data Protection and app sandboxing. Data synced to iCloud is encrypted by Apple and held in the private database of your Apple ID, accessible only to your devices.

Please note: if you export a backup file (.tezbak) and send it through a messaging app or store it in another cloud service, that copy leaves the protections described above, and its security is your responsibility and that of the service you chose.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since the App collects no personal data at all, we hold no data belonging to users of any age.

12. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable laws, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability.

For this App, those rights rest directly with you. We are not the holder of your data: it exists only on your device and in your own iCloud account. You can view, edit, export or delete all of it at any time inside the App, without submitting a request to us or waiting for a reply.

We neither sell nor “share” personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we perform no international transfers — because we receive no personal information in the first place.

13. This website

This website (tripezgo.com) is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, embeds no analytics, and contains no tracking pixels. As with any web host, GitHub may record standard server access logs (such as IP address and browser type) for operational and security purposes; that is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not access or analyse those logs.
